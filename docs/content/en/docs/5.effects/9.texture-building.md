## Introduction

`MaplibreTextureBuilding` renders 3D buildings from the building data given by `source` / `sourceLayer`, applying a `url` image as `fill-extrusion-pattern`; `opacity` / `minzoom` control transparency and the starting zoom level. Requires a tilted view and zoom ≥ `minzoom` (default 15).
