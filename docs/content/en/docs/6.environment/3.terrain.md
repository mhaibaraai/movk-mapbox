## Introduction

`MaplibreTerrain` calls `setTerrain` to enable 3D terrain. The required `source` takes a `raster-dem` source specification (such as public Terrarium-encoded elevation tiles), and `exaggeration` controls how pronounced the relief appears. The effect is most striking with a tilted camera angle.

## Usage

Adjust `exaggeration` to explore the terrain relief around Mount Everest:

::component-example
---
name: TerrainExample
prettier: true
options:
  - name: exaggeration
    label: exaggeration
    default: 1.5
    items:
      - 1
      - 1.5
      - 2
---
::
