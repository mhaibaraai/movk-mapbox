## Introduction

`MaplibreSky` calls `setSky` to configure the sky. The `options` prop is forwarded directly to the MapLibre sky specification (`sky-color`, `horizon-color`, `fog-color`, `sky-horizon-blend`, `horizon-fog-blend`, `fog-ground-blend`, `atmosphere-blend`). Omit it to use the specification defaults; unmounting restores the defaults. The horizon and fog are most visible at high pitch, and `atmosphere-blend` controls the atmosphere glow in globe projection.
