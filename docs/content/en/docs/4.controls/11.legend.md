## Introduction

`MaplibreLegend` shows the legend of every visible [`MaplibreLayerGroup`](/docs/core/layer-group) with a `title`; hiding a group removes its entries. Legend items come from:

- the group's `legend` prop, which takes precedence when set;
- otherwise the colors of the group's layers: a literal color gives one item, `match` gives one item per value, `step` gives one item per range, and `interpolate` gives a gradient. Zoom-driven expressions and other unrecognized expressions are skipped.

The legend is expanded by default and does not collapse on outside clicks; use the button in its top-right corner to collapse it.
