## Introduction

`MaplibreCompare` stacks the `MaplibreMap` in the `#before` and `#after` slots and clips the after map at the divider, so dragging the divider compares the two maps. The cameras of both maps stay in sync (built on [`useMapSync`](/docs/composables/use-map-sync)), and maps inside the slots need no `map-id`. The divider supports pointer dragging and the keyboard (arrow keys, `Home`, `End`).

::note
The after map sits above the before map and is clipped at the divider, so controls inside the after map are only visible in its revealed area. Dock them in a right corner for `vertical` or a bottom corner for `horizontal`.
::
